# 2024 Draft Guide
## Notes:
Don’t draft QBs, RBs, WRs, or TEs from:
- Saints, Seahawks, Titans, Giants, Patriots, Commanders

Do draft QBs, RBs, WRs, or TEs from:
- Lions, Eagles, Colts, Browns, Jets, Falcons, Chiefs, Bills
